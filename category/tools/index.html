Tool holder

  • Screwdriver rack

    Two staggered rows of 12 round holes for screwdrivers, screwed to the wall.

  • Pliers rack

    Six wide slots open to the front; pliers hang by their joint. Keyhole hangers.

  • Wrench rack with sizes

    Ten narrow slots for a metric wrench set, with the size engraved behind each slot.

Drill bit holder

  • Drill bit index

    Ten metric drill bits from 1 to 10 mm in one row, each size engraved under its hole.

  • Screwdriver bit block

    Two rows of 1/4" hex holes for screwdriver bits, labelled with each bit's name.

  • Metric drill set

    Twelve drill bits from 1.5 to 6 mm, including tap drill sizes, in two rows on 3 mm material.

Pegboard & hooks

  • Workshop pegboard

    A 1 inch grid board with J-hooks, pegs and two shelf brackets.

  • Small desk board

    A compact metric board with a glue-on frame and a few pegs for a desk wall.

  • Slotted organiser

    Staggered vertical slots on a 40 mm grid with hooks and shelf brackets.

Cable organizers

  • Labelled cord wraps

    Bone-shaped wraps that clip onto each charger cable, with the device name engraved.

  • Under-desk cable comb

    A comb with six click-in slots and two screw holes, to tidy cables along a desk.

  • Desk edge clips

    Four clips that push onto a 25 mm desk top and hold a cable along the edge.

Spur gears

  • 20:40 gear pair (module 2)

    A 2:1 reduction. The big gear has lightening holes.

  • Single 30-tooth gear

    One module-2 gear with a D-shaft bore.

  • Fidget gear set

    Two chunky 25° gears that spin on pins. Easy to cut, satisfying to turn.

Ruler & protractor

  • 30 cm ruler

    Millimetre scale with engraved centimetre numbers and a hanging hole.

  • 6 inch ruler

    Inch scale down to 1/16, small enough for a pocket.

  • Protractor

    Half-circle protractor, 0–180° with 1° ticks and a window.

  • Set square

    45° triangle with millimetre scales on both legs.

Text stencil

  • Stencil lettering

    Bold slab letters with automatic bridges, ready for spray paint.

  • House number stencil

    Big digits for painting a number on a wall, kerb or bin.

  • Kids alphabet ABC

    Friendly rounded letters on two lines, with a hole to hang it up.
