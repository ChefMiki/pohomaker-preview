Privacy and your data

Last updated 19 October 2026

This page is a draft that is being reviewed. It describes how PoHoMaker works today; the parts marked “To be confirmed” are still being settled.

The short version

PoHoMaker runs in your browser. The designs you make and the files you open are kept in this browser, on this device. They are not sent to PoHoMaker or anyone else. There is no advertising and PoHoMaker sets no cookies. To learn which designs and tools are used, the site counts page visits and a few actions, without cookies and without anything from your designs or files.

What is kept in your browser

  • Your designs (their settings, your additions, their names), and designs in the Trash for 30 days.
  • Files you open in Edit your file, with your edits and their undo steps.
  • Settings such as the theme, units, machine speeds, sheet sizes and editor preferences.
  • Your favourites, stars, folders and a small picture of each design.

They stay until you delete them, or until this browser's data for the site is cleared. Opening a file reads it inside your browser: it is not uploaded.

Usage statistics

The site counts how it is used with PostHog: each page you visit (its address, title and the page you came from, and your browser, screen size and time zone) and a few actions (a design started or exported, a file opened or exported, with their format). Never the names of your designs or files, any text you type, or the files themselves. There is no screen recording, no cookies and nothing kept in your browser for it. If your browser sends “Do Not Track”, nothing is sent. To be confirmed: where PostHog keeps the data, for how long, and that IP addresses are discarded

When your data can be lost

Everything lives only in this browser: clearing the site's data, a private window closing, or another browser or device means it isn't there. Back up everything in My projects downloads one file with all your designs and files; Import brings it back.

Deleting your data

  • One design or file: Delete moves it to the Trash; Delete forever removes it from this browser.
  • Everything in this browser: Delete all my data in My projects.

Your designs belong to you

You keep your rights to the files you open and the designs you make. The bundled fonts are under the SIL Open Font License.
