  1. 24 October 2026House number plates, doors and locks that work, and What you need from the parts

    • NewTwo new fonts: Stardos Stencil, whose cut-out letters keep their middles, and PT Sans Narrow, which has Cyrillic letters.
    • ImprovedThe letters Č, Ć and Đ are drawn even in fonts that don't have them, put together from the font's own letters.
    • FixedUndo takes a paint colour off again.
    • ImprovedThe settings search finds the preview look, its finish, the common thicknesses and paint.
    • ImprovedEdit your file: Delete, Copy, Cut, the arrow keys, Ungroup, Show all and the Order commands say why when there is nothing for them to do. Try it
    • ImprovedDoors, lids and locks are rebuilt so each one opens freely and stays shut: the drop-front cake box's door, the ornament gift box's lid, the barrel bank's end and the letter box's lid.
    • ImprovedA hinged lid stops at about 100° instead of falling back and hanging on its tongues. Try it
    • NewThe letter & number box with a lid can lock with a small padlock: the lid is hinged at one wall and the padlock goes through a tongue at the other. Try it
    • ImprovedWhat you need lists every material a design is cut from, the clear acrylic window too, and the padlock or screws its doors and lids take.
    • ImprovedEdit your file: a file without units says how its size was read and offers the other size with one click, and a sheet border drawn as a cut line becomes a guide. Try it
    • ImprovedSearch finds designs through a typo and everyday words: keychian, dog or engagement. Try it
    • ImprovedExport points to the fit test the first time you use a material, with your material and thickness.
    • NewThickness can now scale the whole drawing for the new thickness, so thicker plywood gives a bigger box, or keep the size as before.
    • ImprovedThe models on design cards turn smoothly and stay inside their card while you scroll, and stop when you ask your system for less motion. Try it
    • ImprovedThe pictures at the top of the app's start page are sharper and load faster. Try it
    • ImprovedA design you open and leave without a change no longer fills My projects.
    • ImprovedOccasion pages show only the designs made for that day, with a few more that suit it under Also good for. Try it
    • ImprovedEvery design has a name of its own: Happy New Year garland, sign and cake topper are three different designs. Try it
    • FixedFine letters and thin bridges are made sturdy enough to cut: a cake topper's script, cut-out names, small snowflakes and pins.
    • FixedA size raised to fit the material shows the size it really cuts, with the reason under it.
    • FixedThe letter box's lid no longer sits in a bent wall for some letters.
    • FixedThe long chapel's door stands on a sill, so nothing under it hangs on a point.
