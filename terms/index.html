What PoHoMaker is

PoHoMaker is a tool in your browser for making laser cutting files: you pick a design, set its sizes and material, and download an SVG or DXF file, or open a file of your own and prepare it for your laser. It is free to use.

Your designs and files

The files you open and the designs you make are yours. PoHoMaker doesn't claim them, and keeps them only where the privacy notice says. To be confirmed: the licence for the templates and what is cut from them, including selling cut products

Cutting safely

A laser cutter can start a fire and some materials give off harmful fumes. You choose the material, check the file and the settings, and watch the machine while it cuts. PoHoMaker's fit test and joint settings help parts fit, but sizes depend on your machine, your material and your settings, so cut a test before a large job. PoHoMaker is not responsible for material, machines or anything damaged while cutting. To be confirmed: the full disclaimer and limitation of liability

Using PoHoMaker

Don't use PoHoMaker to break the law, or to get at other people's data.

Fonts you add

Add only fonts you have the right to use, for example ones you bought or that are free to use. A font you add stays in this browser; PoHoMaker doesn't check font licences and never shares your fonts.
