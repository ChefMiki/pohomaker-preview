Personalise a batch

Personalisation made simple

Scale your business with PoHoMaker. Paste a list of names, check every piece against the real design, and download nested, ready-to-cut sheets: up to 500 pieces at a time.

Personalise a batch
  • Paste names or drop a CSV or TSV file. A quantity column makes copies.
  • Long names shrink, or move to a second line where the design has one.
  • Text that can’t be cut or engraved well is marked before anything is made.
  • Works with every design that has text: 26 of them.
  • Your list stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
